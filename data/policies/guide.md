## 4. Rental Pricing

Each vehicle has a daily rental rate stored in the CRUIS database.

The backend calculates rental prices based on the selected vehicle, rental dates, and requested quantity.

**Rules for the AI assistant:**

- Use the pricing system to provide accurate quotes.
- Never estimate or independently calculate prices.
- Always communicate the price returned by the backend.

The current pricing system does not include seasonal pricing, long-rental discounts, optional extras, additional fees, or sales tax.

## 5. Booking Requirements

- **Minimum age:** 21. Premium vehicles require drivers to be at least 25.
- **Young drivers:** Drivers aged 21 to 24 are subject to a young driver fee.
- **Driving licence:** The driver must hold a valid driving licence for at least one year.
- **International visitors:** Must provide their home-country driving licence. An international driving permit is required if the licence is not in English or Roman characters.
- **At pickup:** The main driver must present a valid photo ID or passport and driving licence.

### Payment

- Customers pay for their rental at the Downtown branch when collecting the vehicle.
- No online payment is required when making a reservation.
- Accepted payment methods: Credit cards, debit cards, and cash.

## 6. Vehicle Availability

Customers can request a vehicle by providing:

- Pickup date
- Return date
- Vehicle category or specific model
- Number of vehicles required

The availability system checks the database and considers existing bookings before returning available vehicles.

**Rules for the AI assistant:**

- Use the availability system to confirm whether a vehicle is available.
- Never promise availability based only on the total fleet quantity.
- If the requested vehicle is unavailable, help the customer check other available categories or models.

## 7. Reservations and Bookings

Customers can request a booking by providing the required booking and customer information.

The booking system stores reservation details in the database. A booking request must be checked against vehicle availability before it is created.

**The AI assistant must:**

- Collect the required booking information.
- Check availability before creating a booking.
- Confirm the booking details with the customer before submission.
- Use the booking system to create the reservation.
- Communicate the result returned by the booking system.

Never tell a customer that a booking has been confirmed unless the backend successfully creates it.

## 8. Cancellations and Late Returns

### Cancellations

- **24 or more hours before pickup:** Free cancellation.
- **Less than 24 hours before pickup:** A fee equal to one day's rental applies.
- **No-show:** A fee equal to one day's rental applies. The booking is held for two hours after the scheduled pickup time before the vehicle is released. Customers who expect to be delayed should contact CRUIS.

### Late Returns

- A 59-minute grace period applies after the scheduled return time.
- After the grace period, each additional hour costs one-third of the daily rental rate, up to a maximum of one additional day's rental.
- If a vehicle is more than 24 hours late without contact, CRUIS may report it as overdue.

## 9. Insurance and Protection

- **Included:** State-minimum third-party liability insurance.
- **Full Protection (optional):** Reduces the renter's financial responsibility for damage to the rental vehicle to $0.
- **Without Full Protection:** The renter is responsible for damage to the vehicle and related costs, including towing, administrative fees, and loss of use while the vehicle is being repaired.
- Some credit cards provide rental vehicle coverage. Customers should check directly with their card provider. CRUIS cannot confirm individual card coverage.

## 10. Accidents, Breakdowns and Emergencies

- If anyone is injured, call **911** first.
- Then contact the CRUIS 24/7 roadside assistance line: **+1 (555) 010-2299**.
- Do not admit fault at the accident scene.
- Take photographs and collect the other driver's details.
- A police report is required for any vehicle damage claim.
- Roadside assistance is free for mechanical breakdowns.
- Assistance involving flat tires, lost keys, or running out of fuel may incur charges.

## 11. Other Fees and Rules

### Additional Fees

- **Smoking in the vehicle:** $250 cleaning fee.
- **Excessive dirt, smells, or pet hair:** $100 cleaning fee.
- **Lost key:** $200.
- **Traffic fines and tolls:** Charged to the renter, plus a $35 administrative fee per fine.

### Vehicle Rules

- Pets must be transported in carriers.
- Additional drivers must be listed on the rental agreement and present at pickup.
- Vehicles must not be taken outside the permitted region.
- Racing, towing, off-road use, and commercial delivery are prohibited.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

**What payment methods do you accept?**

Customers can pay by credit card, debit card, or cash at the Downtown branch when collecting their vehicle. No online payment is required when making a reservation.

**Can someone else pick up the vehicle for me?**

No. The main driver must be present with their identification and driving licence.

**Can I add another driver?**

Yes. Additional drivers must be listed on the rental agreement and present at pickup with their driving licence.

**Can I return the vehicle to a different branch?**

CRUIS currently operates only one branch: Downtown. All vehicles must be returned there.

**What are the opening hours?**

Downtown is open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM and Sunday from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

**What if I have an accident?**

Call 911 first if anyone is injured, then contact the roadside assistance line at +1 (555) 010-2299.

**Do you offer electric vehicles?**

CRUIS's current fleet information does not list electric vehicles.

**What languages do you speak?**

CRUIS customer support is available in English.

## 13. Internal Instructions: Human Assistance and Escalation

**This section is for the AI assistant and is not public customer information.**

The assistant must transfer the conversation to a human when:

- The customer specifically requests human assistance.
- The customer reports an accident, injury, theft, or damage dispute.
- The customer has a complaint or refund dispute.
- The customer reports a payment or security deposit problem.
- The customer asks about something outside the available knowledge base or database.
- The assistant cannot understand or resolve the customer's request after two attempts.

The assistant should briefly explain why human assistance is needed rather than guessing or providing unsupported information.
